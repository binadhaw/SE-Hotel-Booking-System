Privacy notice

What we collect

Your name, username, email and (optionally) phone number; your bookings, payments, reviews, inquiries and trip requests; and, if you choose to give them, your travel preferences (interests, preferred destination and budget).

Why we use it

To manage your account and bookings, pass your booking and inquiry details to the hotel you book with, pass trip requests to the travel agent you choose, and send you booking emails and in-app notifications. Deals and coupons are only emailed if you opted in.

How we protect it
  • Passwords and security answers are stored as one-way BCrypt hashes - nobody can read them.
  • Phone numbers, card holder names and contact details are encrypted in the database (AES-256-GCM).
  • Card numbers and CVVs are never stored - only the card brand and the last four digits.
  • Uploaded payment receipts can only be opened by you, the hotel's manager and administrators.
  • Hotel managers only see bookings and inquiries for their own hotels.
Your choices
  • Edit your details and travel preferences, or turn promotional emails off, on your profile page at any time.
  • Edit or delete your own reviews and inquiries.
  • To close your account, send an inquiry and an administrator will remove it. Booking records are kept for the hotel's accounting, but your login is deleted.
